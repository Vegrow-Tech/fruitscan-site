FAQ

Questions we often hear

My team already checks quality by hand.

Hand checks cover a small sample and depend on who does them. FruitScan measures far more fruit, the same way every time, and keeps the evidence.

Do I need to buy equipment?

On the tree and on the packing line you only need Android phones. Bin Scan uses an external camera.

Will my buyers accept the results?

Orchard reports come with the video each number was measured from, so a buyer can check the work instead of taking it on trust.

Can the orchard scan run on drones?

Yes, the capture setup can be mounted on drones if required. A phone scan is the simpler default: no permits or pilots, and it gets close enough to measure true size.

Does it work on my crop or variety?

Apple runs across all three checkpoints and pomegranate on the tree. New varieties and crops are calibrated before launch.
