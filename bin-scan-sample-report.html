Suggested action for this chamber

Good premium share overall, with a few bins pulling the average down.

  • Sort 3 bins first (C04-B019, C04-B027, C04-B039): defects above 10%, mostly bruising.
  • Watch colour on 2 bins (C04-B011, C04-B047): under 65% of fruit is at least half red.
  • Sell against stock: about 8 t of Extra class fruit is available to commit to premium buyers.
